Wide landscape background for a macOS disk-image installer window, in a five-by-three proportion (about 1.65 times as wide as it is tall). It must match an app icon in which two black holes, one with a glowing amber-gold rim and one with a glowing electric-cyan rim, spiral into each other in a sheet of polished black liquid glass. This background is the quiet, wide continuation of that same world, and it sits behind two icons and their small white labels, so it must stay dark, calm and low in contrast.

MATERIAL. The entire frame is one continuous sheet of polished black liquid glass, like wet obsidian or black lacquer, seen from directly above and perfectly face-on. The material is near black (#07090F) and is revealed only by soft glossy specular highlights along the crests of gentle ripples.

THE SPIRAL. The center of a slow, two-armed gravitational-wave spiral sits at a point about twenty-seven percent of the way in from the left edge and exactly at mid-height. That point and a calm circle around it, about forty percent of the image height across, are flat, smooth, dark glass with no ripples, because the app icon will be placed exactly there. From the edge of that calm circle, exactly two broad, gentle ripple crests spiral outward counterclockwise, interleaved like a two-armed vortex: one crest catches a soft warm amber-gold (#FFB547) highlight and the other a soft electric-cyan (#2EE6FF) highlight. The arms sweep wide across the frame, reaching toward the right side, growing fainter and more widely spaced as they travel, and fading into flat dark glass before the right edge. The highlights are subdued, roughly a third of full brightness, elegant hairline glints rather than neon.

QUIET ZONES. A second calm area of flat dark glass, about forty percent of the image height across, sits at about seventy-three percent of the way in from the left edge at mid-height, where an Applications folder icon will be placed; ripples pass softly around it, not through it. The lower fifth of the frame and the areas just below the two calm circles stay dark and smooth so that small white labels remain readable.

LIGHTING AND COLOR. Two soft raking studio lights, warm amber from the lower left and cool cyan from the upper right, both dim. Strictly amber-gold, cyan, tiny white specular glints and black; no third hue, never a rainbow. Even, dark corners with no bright hotspots or glare.

STYLE. Photorealistic, premium, minimal, like the backdrop of an Apple product page: calm, deep, glossy, sharply focused, low contrast.

DO NOT INCLUDE: black holes, glowing rings, holes or funnels, concentric circles, arrows, text, letters, numbers, icons, folders, windows, stars, galaxies, nebulae, dust, particles, water droplets, lens flares, borders, frames, rounded corners or mockups. Do not add any elements beyond what is described.
